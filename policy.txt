Employees are entitled to 18 paid leave days per year, accrued monthly.
Sick leave is separate from paid leave, capped at 12 days annually.
Unused paid leave can be carried forward to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.
Employees must give at least 3 working days notice before taking planned leave.
Maternity leave is 26 weeks paid, and paternity leave is 2 weeks paid.
Remote work is allowed up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Employees working from a different city for more than 30 days must inform HR.
The probation period for new employees is 3 months, during which no paid leave is allowed except sick leave.
Referral bonus is ₹20,000 for a successful hire, paid after the referred employee completes 3 months.
Expense reimbursements must be submitted within 15 days of the expense, with valid receipts attached.
